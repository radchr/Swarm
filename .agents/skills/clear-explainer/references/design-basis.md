# Design basis (for maintainers)

This skill combines selected practices rather than copying a complete external skill. The external [feynman-technique](https://github.com/guicortei/feynman-technique/blob/main/skills/feynman-technique/SKILL.md) contributes mechanism-before-name, approximation limits, self-audit, and learner teach-back. Its mandatory five-level response ladder and one-analogy rule are not adopted because depth and medium should vary with the reader and task. The local predecessor is preserved at `work/skill-evals/baseline/feynman-stem-explainer` for comparison.

Evidence informing the choices:

- [Fyfe et al., concreteness fading review](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-014-9249-3): concrete-to-abstract transitions can support learning, but the sequence is a design option rather than a universal rule.
- [Gray & Holyoak, analogy and relational mapping](https://reasoninglab.psych.ucla.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/273/2021/12/Gray_Holyoak.2021.pdf): useful analogies preserve relations; surface resemblance alone is insufficient.
- [Rozenblit & Keil, illusion of explanatory depth](https://cogdevlab.yale.edu/sites/default/files/files/rozenblit%20%26%20keil%20%202002.pdf): apparent understanding can collapse when a person has to generate a mechanism.
- [Butler et al., retrieval across varied examples](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29265856/): practice on changed cases helps transfer more than repetition of the same example.
- [Kalyuga et al., expertise reversal](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1207/S15326985EP3801_4): scaffolding that helps novices can burden experienced learners.
- [Mayer & Moreno, multimedia learning](https://carpentries.github.io/instructor-training/files/papers/mayer-reduce-cognitive-load-2003.pdf): relevant visual and verbal elements should align; extra decoration can consume attention.

These sources support design judgments; they do not guarantee that every explanation or artifact improves learning. Behavioral evaluations should ask whether the learner can explain the mechanism and predict a new case, and should review scientific accuracy separately from readability.
