# Pitch Structure & Presentation Contracts
## Layout Patterns, Copy Limits, and Visual Contracts for Cognitive Defense Decks

This reference defines the strict presentation formatting standards for generating investor-grade MilTech and Dual-Use SecOps pitch decks.

---

## 1. Visual Style Contract

* **Slide Dimensions**: 16:9 Widescreen (`13.333` inches width $\times$ `7.5` inches height).
* **Background Color**: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White) on 100% of content slides. Dark slides are strictly forbidden on content pages to ensure maximum printability and corporate rigor.
* **Palette Tokens**:
  * Primary Brand: `#1B3A6B` (Navy Blue) — Title text, category accents, header underline, full-width takeaway banners.
  * Dark Body: `#1E293B` (Slate Dark) — High-contrast body copy, bullet points.
  * Muted Label: `#64748B` (Cool Gray) — Metadata, kickers, slide counters.
  * Card Fill: `#F8FAFC` (Light Slate) — Visual container fill for modular cards.
  * Card Border: `#CBD5E1` (Light Border Gray) — 1pt clean outlines.
  * Highlight Metric: `#2563EB` (Royal Blue) or `#1B3A6B` (Navy) — 18–24pt bold key figures.
  * Alert / Red: `#DC2626` (Adversary / Vulnerability).
  * Safe / Green: `#059669` (Known-Safe / SOTIF verification).
* **Typography**:
  * Headings: `Georgia` (or `Merriweather` / `STKaiti` for bilingual CJK) — 20pt bold.
  * Body Text: `Arial` (or `Calibri`) — 10–11pt regular.
  * Stat Callouts: `Arial` or `Georgia` — 18–28pt bold.

## 2. Layout Pattern Catalog

### Pattern A: 3×2 Grid Cards (Highlights / Governance)
* 6 rounded rectangular cards arranged in 3 columns $\times$ 2 rows.
* Each card contains: Large stat highlight / label, bold 12pt card title, and 2–3 concise bullet lines.
* Best for: Investment Highlights (Slide 2), Empirical Validation (Slide 9), Risk Governance (Slide 16).

### Pattern B: Dual Chart / Split Gap (Problem / Economics / Ask)
* 2 wide side-by-side modules (left: problem/context, right: solution/breakdown).
* Best for: Asymmetric Warfare Problem (Slide 3), Business Model (Slide 11), The Ask & Capital Allocation (Slide 15).

### Pattern C: Comparison Flow / Sequential Stages (Landscape / Roadmap)
* 3–4 horizontal cards connected by process arrows or grouped sequentially.
* Best for: Competitor Mapping (Slide 4), Execution Roadmap (Slide 14).

### Pattern D: Strategic Pillars (Positioning / Target Markets)
* 3 full-width horizontal stacked cards with bold primary label pills on the left.
* Best for: Strategic Positioning (Slide 5), Target Market Segments (Slide 10).

### Pattern E: 3-Column Technical Matrix (Differentiation / Team / Summary)
* 3 vertical columns with distinct headers, technical flow bullet points, and icon tags.
* Best for: Technology Differentiation (Slide 8), Leadership Team (Slide 13), Executive Summary (Slide 17).

### Pattern F: Top/Bottom Architectural Flow (Core Metrology)
* Upper module shows the formal reduction loop (e.g. SOTIF); lower module shows the mathematical formulations ($\text{CCI}, \text{MTOC}, \text{HNP}$).
* Best for: SOTIF & MTOC Metrology (Slide 6).

### Pattern G: Product Toolchain (Portfolio Showcase)
* 3 product cards displaying name, target role, technical stack, and deployment environment.
* Best for: Product Suite (Slide 7).

## 3. Copy & Density Constraints

* **Maximum Words Per Slide**: 60–80 English words (or 35–45 Ukrainian words).
* **One Slide, One Message**: Every slide must have a distinct, singular strategic claim.
* **Mandatory Takeaway Banner**: Every content slide (Slides 2–17) must end with a full-width bottom rectangle (`#1B3A6B`) stating:  
  `  TAKEAWAY: [Direct strategic takeaway in 1 sentence]`
