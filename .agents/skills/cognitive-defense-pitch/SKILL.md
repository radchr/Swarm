# Cognitive Defense Pitch Architect

An elite venture and defense procurement pitch architect for **Cognitive Warfare Defense, Cognitive EDR, and FIMI Countermeasure Technologies**.

This skill synthesizes three core disciplines:
1. **`investor-pitch-planner`**: 6-phase venture narrative (Problem $\to$ Solution $\to$ Market $\to$ Business Model $\to$ Team $\to$ The Ask).
2. **`pitch-deck-creator`**: High-end 18-slide MilTech visual presentation contract (Navy Blue `#1B3A6B`, pure white background `#FFFFFF`, layout patterns A–H, programmatic `.pptx` generation).
3. **`anti-slop` (v3.2)**: Rigorous epistemic filtering — purging "AI magic truth detector" claims, enforcing ISO 21448 SOTIF risk reduction, Lakkaraju bandit triage, MTOC metrology, and Popper falsification matrices.

## 🛡️ The 5 Epistemic Invariants of Cognitive Defense Pitching

Every pitch generated through this skill MUST enforce these five defensive invariants:

1. **No "Autonomous Truth Oracle" Claims**: Never claim the system "detects truth or falsehood universally". Frame the product as an **audited epistemic shield that bounds open-space risk and protects the decision-maker's map from reflexive control**.
2. **ISO 21448 SOTIF Reduction Loop**: Anchor the proving ground / simulation range in the conversion of $\text{Unknown-Unsafe} \to \text{Known-Unsafe} \to \text{Known-Safe}$.
3. **Lakkaraju Bandit Triage (Anti-Fatigue)**: Address the base-rate fallacy ($>90\%$ false alarms in raw feeds) by explicitly formalizing *explore-exploit bandit allocation of human analyst attention*.
4. **MTOC Metrology (Mean Time to Ontological Compromise)**: Provide a concrete, quantifiable resilience metric ($\text{CCI}, \text{MTOC}, \text{HNP}$) analogous to aviation GNSS RAIM integrity monitoring.
5. **Popper Falsification Matrix (Appendix D)**: Every ML/AI subsystem must have an explicit death criterion (fail-fast rule) to eliminate speculative hand-waving.
