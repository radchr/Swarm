# Research and Visual Standard

## Source hierarchy

1. Official program requirements, legislation, standards, doctrine, and first-party datasets.
2. Peer-reviewed research and systematic reviews.
3. Reputable institutional reports with disclosed methods.
4. Preprints, expert commentary, and journalism, labeled by evidence type.
5. Search summaries only as discovery aids, never as final support.

Triangulate high-stakes claims. Record disagreement and uncertainty rather than averaging incompatible findings. A recent source is not automatically a strong source; inspect authorship, venue, method, sample, and citations.

## Cognitive-warfare claim discipline

Distinguish information exposure, psychological response, belief change, behavior change, organizational decision effects, and strategic outcomes. Do not imply causality from correlation or reach metrics. State the unit of analysis, counterfactual, time horizon, confounders, and plausible alternative explanations.

Keep the work defensive and publicly releasable. Avoid personal targeting, covert persuasion recipes, vulnerability exploitation, or operational details that would materially enable manipulation. Prefer resilience, detection, governance, evaluation, and accountable decision support.

## Scientific visual rules

- Encode data honestly; axes, units, denominators, uncertainty, and sample size must remain visible.
- Use colorblind-safe colors and never rely on color alone.
- Use one focal point and one visual grammar per page.
- Preserve at least 40% negative space when possible.
- Keep labels readable in a full-deck thumbnail and on a projected screen.
- Animate to reveal sequence or causality; do not animate decoration.
- For PDF, select the frame that contains the complete inference without requiring motion.
- Add a compact provenance label for adapted figures or datasets.

## Research stack

Use `web-search-pro` for Exa/Tavily/DuckDuckGo discovery. For broad evidence synthesis, route to `deep-research` or `literature-review`; use `scientific-critical-thinking` to inspect evidence quality. Search output can be noisy, so apply the hierarchy above and open the underlying source before citing it.
